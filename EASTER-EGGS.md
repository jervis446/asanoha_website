# Asanoha Easter Eggs

The build includes three brand-themed discovery routes:

1. Type `ASANOHA` anywhere on a page.
2. Click the Asanoha brand mark/logo five times within a short period.
3. Type `OTHERHALF` anywhere on a page.

Each unlocks a small "You found the other half" reveal with links to the Slam Book and Other Half game.

The interaction is local to the visitor's browser and does not generate artificial traffic, votes, or engagement.
