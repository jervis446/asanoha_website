How it works

The plant, the law, the process.

Everything that happens between “I’m curious” and a pair of tins at your door, explained without the legal jargon.

01

The plant

Vijaya is Ayurveda’s name for the cannabis leaf. We use only the leaf, never the flowers or resin, as a full spectrum extract that keeps the plant’s natural range of compounds.

  • Leaf only, as Indian law requires
  • Full spectrum extract
  • Strength printed on every label
02

The law

Cannabis leaves sit outside the NDPS Act’s definition of prohibited cannabis. Leaf-based Ayurvedic proprietary medicines are made under an AYUSH manufacturing licence and sold on prescription.

  • AYUSH manufacturing licence
  • Schedule E(1) and caution on every pack
  • Adults 18+ only, prescription always
03

The process

Made under licence, tested batch by batch, packed in pairs, and shipped only after a registered practitioner’s prescription is verified.

  • Certificate of Analysis for each batch
  • Plain, sealed packaging
  • Delivery across India

Your order, step by step

Four steps, zero awkwardness.

  1. Pick your pairGummies, oil or unTrippy tablets, in the strength your practitioner suggests. Add a slam book line if you like.
  2. Share a prescriptionUpload one from a registered Ayurvedic practitioner, or tick “I need a consultation” and we’ll help you book an online one.
  3. We verify, you payWe check the prescription, then send your price and a secure payment link on WhatsApp or email.
  4. It arrives, in pairsPlain, sealed packaging with tracking. An adult signs for it. The free rolling paper booklet comes along for the ride.
