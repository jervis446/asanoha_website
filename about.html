About us

Three friends, one idea, far too many voice notes.

Asanoha started the way most good ideas in India start: over chai, after a long day, with someone saying “yaar, hear me out.”

Adarsh had the idea. Joseph asked how everyone felt about it. Soumyadev asked what it would cost, who would do the paperwork, and whether any of us had eaten. Between an MBA at IIM Indore, two day jobs and Bangalore traffic, we built this one weekend at a time.

The honest reason behind it is simpler. We missed people. School friends in other cities. College friends in other time zones. The friend who stayed when things were hard, and whom you haven’t called in four months because work. We wanted to make something that comes with a built-in reason to reach out. So every pack comes in two halves, and the second half is never for you.

Adults only, and only with a practitioner’s prescription. Being fun doesn’t mean being careless.
