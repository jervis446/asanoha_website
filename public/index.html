01 The problem

Every hire, every answer, every payment: someone takes a cut.

In India, getting hired, getting a straight answer and getting paid each pass through a middleman who charges at every step.

  • Hiring. A recruiter pays to see resumes, and a job seeker hands their phone number to everyone who asks.
  • Answers. Someone who wants a straight answer pays by the minute.
  • Payments. A small seller loses a slice of every sale.

03 What people have tried

Everyone gets by. The middle still gets paid.

  • Recruiterssearch by keyword and read resumes by hand. Skills tests start at US$79 a month. HackerRank pricing, 2026
  • Job seekersapply everywhere and share their phone number with everyone.
  • People who want guidancepay for per-minute chats and get answers they cannot check.
  • Small sellerspay a gateway, or show a bare UPI QR code with no receipts, refunds or wallet.

04 The reveal

Asanoha: one account, one payment rail and one wallet, with apps on top.

We build the shared parts once and publish the payment code. Every app on top starts with them, so none of them needs a middleman for sign-in, payments or refunds.

A recruiter at a small agency needs one backend developer.

The other choice is a bare UPI QR code, with no receipts, refunds or wallet.

Yes, we charge too.

Questions

What people ask us

How do you make money?

Flat prices, shown before anyone pays: People Pitchers seats, Kosmik passes and report packs, and store orders once the store opens. Later, a hosted Noha Pay for other sellers at a flat monthly price. Today only Kosmik passes and reports take money.

Isn’t that unfocused?

The focus is the shared layer under the apps. Sign-in, payments, the wallet, WhatsApp messages and AI caching were built once, so a new app starts with all of them. Our measure is how fast a new idea reaches paying users and how little it costs to run. Apps that don’t earn their place will be shut down, not kept alive.

Why now?

UPI made small payments cheap to take, AI lets a small team build what once needed a large one, and Indian users now expect apps in their own language.

Who are you up against?

In hiring, Naukri and LinkedIn, and test makers such as HackerRank and Mettl. In guidance, Astrotalk and other per-minute apps. In payments, gateways such as Razorpay. Each is large. We start with the part they leave to a middleman or a per-minute fee.

Why you?

We ship. 558 commits since 25 September, four products and a registered LLP, all alongside day jobs. One founder writes the code, one handles people and partners, and one runs operations.

What are the risks?

We have not yet shown that people will pay at scale: ₹102 so far. The store will sell a regulated Ayurvedic medicine, only on prescription, so it opens only after legal sign-off. WhatsApp codes run through our own relay today and should move to the official WhatsApp Business API before we scale. And several apps is a lot for three people with day jobs, which is why they share everything they can.

What happens after I get in touch?

You confirm your WhatsApp number with a code, so we know the message is really from you. We get your note straight away, and one of the founders writes back to you.
