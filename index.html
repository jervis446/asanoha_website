Our story

Every good thing in our lives came in halves.

Half a samosa at the school canteen. Half the rent in our first Bangalore flat. Half a cab home at 1 a.m., split three ways because somebody always “forgot” their wallet.

Then came jobs, cities and calendar invites, and the halves quietly stopped. Friends turned into a WhatsApp group that only wakes up for birthdays. You know the one. You probably muted it.

Asanoha is our small protest against that. Every pack comes as two: one for you, one for the friend you keep meaning to call. Inside is Vijaya, Ayurveda’s old name for the cannabis leaf, made properly by an AYUSH-licensed manufacturer, prescribed by a real practitioner, and packed like a gift you’d actually want to give.

The name comes from asanoha, an old Japanese hemp-leaf pattern that stands for growing straight and strong. The leaf in our logo is split down the middle, clay on one side and gold on the other, because nothing here is meant to be whole until someone else holds the other half.

Adarsh, Joseph and Soumyadev

FAQ

Things people ask us at weddings.

Is this legal in India?

Yes. Our products are Ayurvedic proprietary medicines made only from Vijaya (cannabis) leaf extract by an AYUSH-licensed manufacturer. Cannabis leaves are outside the NDPS Act’s definition of prohibited cannabis, and the Ministry of AYUSH advises that such medicines be sold on prescription, which is exactly how we sell them.

Do I really need a prescription?

Yes, for every order. Upload one from a registered Ayurvedic practitioner at checkout. If you don’t have one yet, pick “I need a consultation” and we’ll help you book one before anything ships.

Which strength should I choose?

The one your practitioner prescribes. Most people start with Low, the beach tins. Medium and High are for when your practitioner decides to step you up. unTrippy tablets carry 250 mg of extract each and sit with our high-strength range.

Will it make me feel high?

Leaf extract can contain small amounts of THC, and people respond differently. Take only what’s prescribed, skip alcohol with it, and don’t drive or operate machinery until you know how it affects you. These are medicines, not party favours.

Can I gift the second tin?

That’s the whole idea, with one rule: the person using it needs their own prescription. If your friend doesn’t have one yet, gift them the empty tin later. It makes a very good pencil box.

Who shouldn’t take it?

Anyone under 18, anyone pregnant or breastfeeding, and anyone whose doctor says no. If you take other medicines, tell your practitioner before you start.

How does delivery and payment work?

Send your order on WhatsApp or email from checkout. Once we verify your prescription, we share a secure payment link and ship across India in plain, sealed packaging with tracking.

Can I return it?

Medicines can’t be returned once delivered, but if anything arrives damaged, wrong or expired, tell us within 48 hours with photos and we’ll replace it or refund you. Full details are in our terms.
